Mauritius Life: a free multiplayer life game set in Mauritius

Mauritius Life is a free life simulation you play in your web browser, on your phone or your computer, together with real players. The world follows the real island of Mauritius, built from OpenStreetMap data, and opens one region at a time, starting with Grand Baie. There is nothing to download and nothing to pay.

Play Mauritius Life

What you can do

  • Build a life. Pick your look, find a job, earn coins and climb the career ladder.
  • Live in a home. Buy a house or flat, move the furniture and invite friends over.
  • Cook and look after yourself. Make meals from noodles to a full dinner, keep your stamina up and handle surprises like running out of gas.
  • Go out. Party, work out at the gym, race, play at the Grand Casino and ride free electric scooters around town.
  • Make friends, and drama. Chat with the town, send messages, borrow and lend money, flirt, fall out and make up.
  • Join Saturday's Fashion Show. Enter a look and vote for your favourite on the runway.
  • Say what is on your heart. The Heart Garden lets you post an anonymous note for the whole town to read.
  • Come back every day. A daily reward grows with your streak, and you and a friend both get coins when they join through your invite link.

Play anywhere

Mauritius Life runs in any modern browser. Add it to your home screen for a full-screen app feel.

Questions

Is it free? Yes. Coins are earned in the game.

Do I need an account? No. Pick a name and start playing.

Is it multiplayer? Yes. Everyone you see walking around town is a real person.
