Gift money

💬 Flic en Flac Team inbox

Messages players send to the Flic en Flac Team chat in their phone. Open one to read it and reply. Your reply appears in their Messages straight away.

✂️ A* Cutz bookings

Haircut bookings made in the game (Tamarin barber shop). Confirm or decline them and the player is told in the game. Booked guests also get the discount in the game from half an hour before to two hours after their time, and the A* Cutz Regular medal on their first booking.

💬 Business chats

Send a message from a business (Belle's Kitchen, A* Cutz) to every player. It lands in their Messages as a chat from that business, never in public chat. Players can write back and get an automatic reply with the WhatsApp number.

🎃 Halloween Night

Trick or treat candy, the costume contest and the ghost hunt. Prizes are paid automatically when it ends: costume contest Rs 50,000 / 25,000 / 10,000 and candy leaderboard Rs 30,000 / 15,000 / 5,000. A message from "Halloween Night" goes to every player's Messages when it starts and when it ends.

🗺️ Treasure hunt

The Rs 15,000,000 Halloween treasure hunt (5 clues). It runs while Halloween Night runs. The first player to dig up the chest wins and the prize is sent to their mail. If you turn auto-pay off, the winner waits here until you press Pay the winner.

📊 Polls

Send a question to the whole game. Players see it as a card when they log in (once, until they vote), under Settings → Poll, and get a team announcement. One vote each. After voting they see the results.

🚗 Student driver trips

Hidden from all players until you switch it on. Add testers by player name to try it first. Drivers must be verified here before passengers can see them. Check their licence or learner permit before you verify.

🍛 Belle's Kitchen bookings

Table bookings made in the game. Confirm or decline them, and the player is told in the game. Booked guests get the discount from half an hour before their table until two hours after.

🗺️ Island regions

Locked regions are grey on the map and the server refuses anyone who tries to go in (on foot, by car, by ride or by air). Opening one takes effect straight away, no deploy. Closing one moves everyone inside to a safe open place. Set a date and the region opens by itself at that time.

🌀 Natural disasters

Players get a two minute warning, then screen shake, rain or water, a rumble and a repair bill that grows with wealth (nothing under Rs 500k; 1% / 3% / 6% of each band above that, 30% goes to the town treasuries). Automatic disasters start once Season 2 is live, one every 24 to 48 hours.

🏁 Seasons

📣 Ads

How the clients' ads in the game are doing. Seen = a player came near it, Opened = they tapped the billboard or opened the app, Clicked = they pressed the button to the client's site.

⚖️ Seizure requests

The cheat watch never takes money by itself. When it sees cash with no recorded source it adds a request here, and nothing happens until you decide. Approve takes the money (it leaves their cash the next time they play, never below zero) and can send it to a town treasury. Decline leaves their money alone. Look at their Money sources first if you are not sure.

💸 Transfers that never arrived

Until now the game threw away any single transfer over Rs 10,000,000 when it arrived. They are listed here from the players' private chats. Press Pay to send the money to the player who never got it (it arrives in their mail). Transfers from now on are cut into parts the game accepts, so this cannot happen again.

🕵️ Seized money

Every seizure, by you or by the cheat watch, newest first. Press Return to give all of it back to the player (it arrives in their mail), or Return part to choose an amount. For admin seizures the town treasury gives back what it still has. Search by player name to see only theirs.

🏢 Businesses and investments

The game keeps a history of every player's businesses, investments, jet, cars and home. Below are the players who recently lost something, so you can find them fast. Open a player (or press "Assets" on their row in Players) to see every snapshot and restore their businesses, investments or other assets, without touching their cash. Their game loads the restore by itself.

🕵️ Suspected players

Checks every player's history: money with no recorded source, big jumps in net worth, income that is mostly crime, gambling or gifts, and huge single lines. Each one gets a score and the reasons. Open Money sources to look closer.

💳 Transactions

Every money line the players' games report: wages, purchases, transfers, rent, taxes, fines, winnings, and anything else that changes a balance. Search by player name, username or what it was for. Click a player's name to see only theirs.

🏴‍☠️ Raids

Players worth over the limit, offline, with enough cash can be raided for 3%, 6% or 10% of their net worth. The tax, crew fees, search fees and defense upgrades all go to the town treasury.

💰 Wealth tax

Every Monday, players pay this share of their net worth above each line: from cash first, then savings. The money goes to the town treasury where they are. Rates are percentages (0 to 20).

Seize money

Takes cash from this player the next time they play (never below zero) and puts it in a town treasury. It is based on their last saved cash.

Investigate player

Opens a Financial Crimes case and freezes part of their money until the court rules. The whole town sees the announcement. They can defend themselves in the Court app, or you can rule on it below.

🕵️ Financial Crimes agent

Watches how players get rich (theft, winnings, transfers) and audits the richest player every few hours. Assets are frozen until the court rules. Release a player here if a case looks wrong.

📊 Stats

Site visits

Everyone who opens the site, even if they never start playing. A visitor is one browser a day. Bots and link previews are left out. Counting started today; older days show 0.

Where visitors come from

Page views in the last 30 days, by the ?ref= tag or the website that linked here. “direct” means typed in, a bookmark or an app that hides the source.

Coming back

Of the players active on a day, how many played again the next day, exactly 7 days later, or on any of the next 7 days. Days are Mauritius time.

Busiest times

Most players online at once in each hour, averaged over the last 7 days. Hover a bar for details. Schedule events in the tallest hours.

Where players come from

New players by the ?ref= tag on the link they first opened. “direct” means no tag. Visits count every time someone opens a tagged link (last 30 days).

Links to share

Use a different link in each place, so you can see which one works. Tap to copy.

Banned

Delete player?

This permanently removes:

  • their character, money and progress
  • their login (username and password)
  • their house plot and home invites
  • money transfers waiting for them, and gossip about them

If they're online they're kicked out now. Next time their browser opens the game it starts a new character. A backup of all data is saved first.

New password

Tell the player this password in private. They log in with their username and this password. Any old password stops working.
