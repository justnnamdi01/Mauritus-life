# Mauritius Life: launch plan and marketing kit

## Goal and audience
- **Goal:** 1,000 sign-ups in the first week, 30% still playing on day 7, and a core of players who invite friends.
- **Who:** Mauritians aged 15 to 30 on phones, plus the diaspora. They live on WhatsApp, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram.
- **Promise:** "Your life, your island. Start with nothing, end up on the Rich list."

## Timeline
| When | What |
|---|---|
| T-30 days | Region 1 (Grand Baie) playable by the team. Domain, hosting, share image, icons. |
| T-21 | Closed test with 20 friends on real phones. Fix what breaks. |
| T-14 | Teaser video (15 s). Open the waitlist: a WhatsApp group or a form. |
| T-7 | Founders' codes: the first 200 get a Founder badge and a small starting bonus. Gameplay video (30 s). |
| T-3 | Daily countdown posts. Creator invites (see the brief below). |
| T-0 | Launch video (30 s). Launch event in game: the **Founders' Race** (first 100 to reach a set net worth win a badge and a prize). |
| T+1 to 14 | One post a day. Share the top of the Rich list. Fix bugs fast and post the "What's new". |
| T+30 | Season 1 event (reuse the treasure hunt or boss templates) and the Region 2 reveal. |

## Channels
| Channel | Use |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp (Status, groups) | The main engine. Short text plus the 15 s teaser. |
| TikTok and Reels | The videos below, plus screen recordings of real play. |
| Facebook groups | Mauritian communities, student groups. Ask the admins first. |
| Local creators | 5 to 10 small creators: free Founder accounts, a prize for their followers. |
| In game | Invites (the game already rewards both sides) and the Rich list as bragging rights. |

## Launch-day mechanics
- **Founders' Race:** a public leaderboard for launch week, a badge for the first 100.
- **Invite rewards:** keep the existing invite system, cap the daily bonus so it cannot be farmed.
- **Server check before launch:** nothing important deployed on launch day, backups verified, the admin page tested, rate limits tested with 200 test connections.

## Video production (Higgsfield)
- **Do the videos after Region 1 is playable.** Real footage plus a cartoon intro is more believable than a cartoon alone.
- **Style line to reuse in every prompt:** "Polished stylized 3D cartoon animation, warm golden-hour colours, soft shading, chunky rounded characters with warm brown skin and simple dot eyes, a cute Mauritian beach town, red-roofed shops with striped awnings, palm trees, turquoise sea, game interface overlays: a rounded HUD, a minimap, a chat box, white floating name pills."
- **Rules we learned:** one clip per scene (5 to 8 s), keep on-screen text to a few short words and add the real title in your editor, always attach the mascot reference image, decline unrelated preset suggestions.
- **Costs (credits):** a 30 s 480p draft is about 90, a 30 s 720p final about 210. The first Flic en Flac clips used about 290 credits in total.

## Numbers to watch (admin page)
Sign-ups per day, day-1 and day-7 return rate, invites sent and accepted, ads and posts tracked in `game/ADS.md` (add a row for every post, ad, poster or poll), top Rich list changes, crashes.

## Launch checklist (from our mistakes)
- [ ] Every event area is open to all players (not locked to a test account).
- [ ] Announcement wording does not say who will lose money. Give players something to choose and buy.
- [ ] No deploy during an event if it can be avoided. If you must, post a warning first.
- [ ] Tested on at least 3 different real phones, including a slow one.
- [ ] The real title is spelled correctly on every video, added in an editor.
- [ ] A backup restore was tried once.
