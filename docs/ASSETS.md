# Character models we still need, and where to look (10 Nov 2026)

We need more everyday clothes and hair for both men and women that the current free Quaternius "Ultimate Modular" pack lacks: **swimwear, formal wear, traditional and modest wear (sari, salwar kameez, hijab and head scarves), and natural hair textures (afro, braids, curls)**. Nothing in this file has been downloaded or put in the game. The two Quaternius pages were read directly on 10 Nov 2026 (see the verified section); the other rows come from web search results and still need checking on their own pages.

## Verified on the Quaternius pages (10 Nov 2026)
- **Universal Base Characters** (Aug 2025, quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html): 6 base models in Superhero, Regular and Teen proportions (male and female), **20 hairstyles**, a Humanoid rig, FBX and glTF (and OBJ, Blend), 26 files, **CC0** ("Free to use in personal, educational and commercial projects"), average **13,000 triangles per character**, no generative AI. Standard download is a 122 MB zip on itch.io (quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters); the Source version (shaders for eye and skin colour, rigged .blend files) is paid or Patreon. The page does not list the hairstyles by name, so we do not yet know if they include afro, braids or curls.
- **Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy** (Nov 2025): 12 outfits, 62 modular parts, 3 colour textures each, fits the Universal Base Characters' heads, Humanoid rig, glTF, **CC0**. Fantasy costumes only.
- **What this means for us:** these use a different (Humanoid) skeleton from our current Ultimate Modular pack, so using them means switching the whole character system or re-rigging pieces, and 13,000 triangles per person is heavy for a crowd on a phone (our current pieces are lighter). It gives more body shapes and 20 hairstyles but **no swimwear, formal or traditional clothes**. So the commission for the missing pieces is still needed, and it should be made for whichever skeleton we choose.

## Paid, ready-made (CGTrader and similar, usually royalty-free but not CC0)
Swimwear packs (about 2 to 50 USD, some with glb), a low-poly salwar kameez (fbx, obj, glb, blend), a sari pack ("no AI" royalty-free), a hijab character (about 10 USD; marked as AI-assisted, which may matter), and afro and braided hair models. Licence terms vary: read each one before buying.
