# Character models we still need, and where to look (10 Nov 2026)

We need more everyday clothes and hair for both men and women that the current free Quaternius "Ultimate Modular" pack lacks: **swimwear, formal wear, traditional and modest wear (sari, salwar kameez, hijab and head scarves), and natural hair textures (afro, braids, curls)**. Nothing in this file has been downloaded or put in the game. The session could not open quaternius.com (the network blocks it), so everything below comes from web search results and has to be checked on the real pages before anyone relies on it.

## Free options found (check the licence on each page)
| Source | What it offers | Licence (as reported) | Fit for us |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quaternius, Universal Base Characters | Many character bodies (the page reportedly lists 26 models; another listing says 6) and a set of hairstyles (reported 8 to 20), glTF and FBX | CC0 per its page; Quaternius now also publishes a "QAL v1.0" licence (free commercial use, no reselling as a pack) | Best base for new bodies and hair. Needs checking: skin tones, whether hair includes afro and braids |

| KayKit Adventurers | Five rigged characters | CC0 | Costume style, not everyday |
| Kenney (blocky and mini characters) | Simple animated characters | CC0 | Wrong style |
| Mixamo (Adobe) | Free rigged characters and animations | Free with an Adobe account for use in games, not for redistributing the files | Possible for animation; not a clothing set |
| "Low Poly People Pack" (itch.io) | 12 characters, 6 bodies with different clothes and hair | CC BY-SA 4.0, not CC0 (share-alike means changes must be shared) | Probably not suitable |
| M3 Character Studio (open source, MIT) | A web tool to assemble glTF/VRM avatars from parts you bring | MIT | Only a tool; it ships no hijab or afro assets |

## Paid, ready-made (CGTrader and similar, usually royalty-free but not CC0)
Swimwear packs (about 2 to 50 USD, some with glb), a low-poly salwar kameez (fbx, obj, glb, blend), a sari pack ("no AI" royalty-free), a hijab character (about 10 USD; marked as AI-assisted, which may matter), and afro and braided hair models. Licence terms vary: read each one before buying.

## Recommended plan
1. **Get the Quaternius Universal Base Characters and read its licence page** (needs quaternius.com allowed on the network, or download it by hand on a computer and send me the files). Check how many skin tones, body shapes and hairstyles it has, and whether it includes natural hair textures.
2. **Fill the gaps with a small commission** (a 3D artist makes 6 to 10 pieces in the same low-poly style and the same skeleton): swim trunks and swimsuit, formal suit and dress, sari, salwar kameez, hijab and head scarf, and 4 hair textures (afro, braids, curls, locs). Ask for glb files rigged to our skeleton, CC0 or full commercial rights, no AI-generated models.
3. **Add them to the game** as new pieces (same file name pattern `body-NAME.glb`, `legs-`, `head-`, `feet-`) and add them to the clothing list; this is about a day of work once the files exist.
